02 — The taxonomy

The answer was in the webhook the whole time.

Every failed payment Razorpay sends you carries four fields describing exactly why it failed. Read them, and the right recovery action follows. Ignore them, and every failure looks the same — so every response is the same.

A hard decline means the issuing bank refused the card outright. Retrying it hits the same bank with the same card and gets the same answer — which is why the taxonomy points at a different payment method, not a second attempt. The classifier reached this deterministically, with no model involved.

03 — What it's worth

Choosing the action beats choosing the case.

Measured on — held-out simulated cases under an identical contact budget of —. Every arm works the same cases and spends the same contacts, so the only variable is the decision.

Baseline: a fixed retry at 24 hours — Razorpay's own documented subscription retry policy, and what most recovery tooling does by default.

And ranking the queue is worth almost nothing.

Ranking by expected value instead of by amount — using oracle uplift, the ceiling rather than our estimate. Amounts span roughly 5,000× while uplift spans 5×, so the product barely reorders the queue.

04 — What we refused to build

Three things that didn't work, kept in.

These are measurements that went against the design. Removing them would have made the numbers above look better and mean less.

A per-case ML model lost to a group-by

A gradient-boosted uplift model, properly calibrated, came in 24% worse than a twenty-row average over cause and action. The model stayed out of the decision path.

The allocator is worth ~0 on revenue

Against a pooled agent handed a value-sorted queue it wins by about 1%. It is in the system as a governance layer — budgets, quiet hours, approval — not as a revenue engine, and it is reported that way.

The model can't beat the noise floor

Outcomes are Bernoulli draws, so ROC-AUC here is capped at 0.637. Ours reaches 0.620 — 88% of what is achievable. The remaining gap is not signal anyone can capture.
